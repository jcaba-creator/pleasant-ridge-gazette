Engine Oil & Filter Replacement
Capacity: 5.2 - 5.7 qts 5W-30 • Filter: Motorcraft FL-910S
30-45m
Drive onto ramps or position front jack stands; engage parking brake and chock rear tires.
Drop lower belly pan undertray: remove four 8mm screws and three plastic push-clips.
Position 10+ quart drain pan under oil pan drain plug. Remove drain plug with 15mm box wrench. Allow 15 min hot drain.
Inspect magnetic drain plug for excessive swarf. Clean threads thoroughly. Reinstall with fresh crush gasket and torque to 20 lb-ft (27 Nm). Do not over-torque aluminum pan threads.
Loosen Motorcraft FL-910S filter. Catch drain overflow. Clean filter mounting boss on engine block.
Lightly coat rubber gasket of new FL-910S with clean 5W-30 oil. Spin on until gasket makes contact with face, then hand-tighten an additional 3/4 to 1 full turn. Never use wrench to tighten.
Pour in 5.2 quarts of Motorcraft SAE 5W-30 Full Synthetic (WSS-M2C961-A1). Check dipstick; top off toward 5.7 qts maximum line as needed.
Start engine and idle for 2 minutes. Check drain plug and filter perimeter for leaks. Reinstall undertray.
IOLM Reset: Turn ignition ON (engine off). In cluster steering controls: Menu > Settings > Vehicle Maintenance > Oil Life > Hold OK until display resets to 100%.
Tire Rotation & Cold Inflation
Pattern: Forward Cross / Modified X • Cold Pressure: 35 PSI
30m
Loosen lug nuts 1/2 turn using 21mm socket while tires are on ground.
Raise Explorer safely onto 4 heavy-duty jack stands positioned under reinforced factory lift notches.
AWD / RWD Rotation Pattern: Front tires move straight back to the rear on the same side. Rear tires cross over to opposite front corners (Rear Left → Front Right; Rear Right → Front Left).
Inspect brake pads through caliper inspection window (minimum friction material 3mm). Wire-brush light surface rust from wheel hub centering ring.
Mount wheels. Hand-thread all M14x1.5 lug nuts. Snug in star pattern.
Lower vehicle to ground. Using calibrated torque wrench, tighten in progressive star pattern to 100 lb-ft (baseline ref) / 150 lb-ft (204 Nm) (OEM CD6 M14 factory specification).
Check cold tire pressure across all four road tires to 35 PSI. Check compact spare tire to 60 PSI.
Mandatory OPSEC Check: Recheck and retorque all wheel lug nuts within 100 miles of driving.
Spark Plug Service (CYFS-12Y-PT)
Motorcraft SP-578 • Gap: 0.028 - 0.031 in • Torque: 133 lb-in
45-60m
Pre-Condition: Engine must be completely COLD to prevent aluminum cylinder head thread galling or stripping. Disconnect 12V AGM negative battery terminal.
Remove plastic engine decorative cover by pulling up firmly on rubber mounting grommets.
Carefully release red locking slide tabs on coil-on-plug (COP) electrical connectors. Disconnect all 4 harness plugs.
Remove the 8mm retaining bolt from each ignition coil. Twist and pull coils upward. Inspect rubber boots for carbon tracking or oil seepage from valve cover gasket.
Blow compressed air down each spark plug tube to clear loose grit and sand before plug removal.
Using 5/8" magnetic spark plug socket and 6" extension, unthread old spark plugs.
Inspect new Motorcraft SP-578 (CYFS-12Y-PT) plugs. Verify electrode gap with wire feeler gauge to 0.028 – 0.031 in (0.7 – 0.8 mm). Do not force or lever against fine iridium tip.
Thread plugs in entirely by hand using the socket extension to guarantee zero cross-threading.
Torque each plug to 133 lb-in (11 lb-ft / 15 Nm) with an inch-pound torque wrench. Never use impact or uncalibrated ratchets.
Apply a pea-sized dab of dielectric grease inside the tip of each coil boot. Reinstall coils, snug 8mm bolts to 71 lb-in (8 Nm), reconnect harness clips, and reinstall engine cover.
Engine & Cabin Air Filter Replacement
Engine: FA-1884 • Cabin: FP-89
15-20m
Engine Air Filter (FA-1884): Locate airbox on driver's side front of engine compartment. Release the two metal spring clamps on front edge of housing. Lift airbox lid, withdraw old pleated element. Vacuum leaf debris from bottom of airbox housing. Seat new FA-1884 filter, ensuring perimeter sealing rubber fits into housing groove. Re-engage rear alignment tabs and snap clamps closed.
Cabin Air Filter (FP-89): Open glove box. Empty contents. Unhook the slow-drop string damper on the right outer side. Squeeze both side walls inward to disengage travel stops and let glove box swing down fully.
Locate cabin filter access door. Squeeze release tabs on both ends of cover and pull cover forward.
Slide old filter out horizontally. Note direction of airflow arrow (MUST POINT DOWNWARD toward blower impeller). Slide in fresh Motorcraft FP-89 filter. Snap access door securely. Re-engage glove box stops and reconnect dampener arm.
10-Speed Transmission Service (10R60/10R80)
Fluid: Motorcraft MERCON ULV (XT-12-QULV) • Service Fill: ~6.5 qts
1-2h
CRITICAL OPERATIONAL MANDATE: This 10-speed transmission requires Motorcraft MERCON ULV. Under NO circumstances use MERCON V, MERCON LV, or universal multi-vehicle ATF. The low viscosity formula is essential for valve body solenoids; incorrect fluid causes clutch pack failure.
Level vehicle on 4-post hoist or 4 jack stands with spirit level on transmission pan rail.
Loosen transmission pan perimeter bolts slowly from rear to front to tilt pan and catch draining fluid in wide catch tub (~6.0 to 7.0 quarts will drain).
Remove pan and internal filter. Clean round pan magnet of fine clutch dusting. Clean pan mating surface. Install fresh OEM transmission filter and new pan gasket.
Torque transmission pan bolts evenly in crisscross pattern to 106 lb-in (12 Nm).
Refill through transmission case fill port / dipstick cap on right side using hand pump and 6.0 qts fresh MERCON ULV.
Temperature Check Procedure: Connect OBD-II scanner. Start engine and idle through P-R-N-D gear selections holding each for 5 seconds. Warm transmission fluid until TFT reads between 195°F and 205°F (91°C – 96°C). With engine running in Park, check fluid level against mini-dipstick crosshatch. Top off precisely to hot mark.
