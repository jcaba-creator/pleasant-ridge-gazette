Project K9-Volt

16-Level Cyber-Corgi Platformer • The 449 Field Arcade

A rogue mutagen centrifuge has infected the facility! Play as K9-Volt across 16 challenging levels. Every level introduces a realistic mutant animal breed with glowing red eyes until cleansed! Defeat the Titan Great Dane Boss on Level 16 to unlock the Secret Sector 17 and free your trapped family!

Controls:
• A/D / ←→ : Run (Build Sonic Zoomies)
• W/Space : Jump • J : Bark Wave
• K : Swap Element (Pyre, Hydro, Flora)
• Shift / P / E / S + J : Super Mega Power Poot
Tactical Directives:
• PYRE > FLORA > HYDRO > PYRE
• Running 3.0s activates Mach-Bark Zoomies (shatters lab containers on contact!)
• Level 16 Exit Airlock is LOCKED until the Boss is de-mutated!
• Defeating the Boss unlocks Secret Level 17 via ENTER!
