Policy & Benefits FAQ
Can the school require 40 hours?
No. Time schedules for classified employees are assigned by administration (Board Policy GCA). Part-time classified roles between 20 and 30 hours are standard across Kansas districts.
How does the 6% KPERS money work?
It is a mandatory savings account: 6% is taken from your monthly paycheck, and it gains 4% interest while it's in the account. When you leave the school, you take it with you as a refund or rollover.
Do part-time employees get paid sick days?
Yes. Once contracted for 20 or more hours per week, employees accrue 12 paid chargeable leave days per school year (Handbook p. 8), usable for illness or personal leave.
Is there any penalty for working 32 to 35 hours?
No financial penalties. Take-home pay increases slightly (~$11.26/hr net on extra hours) and monthly fringe increases to $185, but the shift extends further into the afternoon.
