📋 Getting Started Checklist Prerequisites
1. Hunter Education Certificate:
Mandatory for all hunters born on or after July 1, 1957. Anyone 16+ may purchase a one-time Apprentice License without hunter ed (valid 1 year while accompanied).
2. Licenses & Tags (gooutdoorskansas.com):
• KS Resident Annual Hunt License: $27.50
• Resident Deer Permit (Any-Season Whitetail): $42.50
• Waterfowl Requirements: State Waterfowl Stamp ($10), Federal Duck Stamp ($25), plus free annual HIP certification.
3. Daily Electronic Check-In (iSportsman):
Mandatory daily check-in/out via smartphone or automated call for all KDWP Wildlife Areas and Fort Leavenworth prior to entering fields.
4. Hunter Orange Requirement:
Mandatory during firearm deer seasons: minimum 100 sq inches visible on front and back, plus orange hat.

2. Fort Leavenworth Installation Managed Units
⏱️ 23 min (14.0 mi E via K-92)
• Units: Designated bluff and river-bottom timber.
• Rules: Post firearm registration with Provost Marshal, Fort Leavenworth iSportsman account, safety harness mandatory for tree stands. Units open around military training schedules.
3. Perry Wildlife Area (Valley Falls / Ozawkie)
⏱️ 35 min (26.0 mi W via K-92 / K-4)
• Size: 11,000+ acres public land around Perry Reservoir.
• Game: Premier waterfowl marshes, deer, turkey, quail.
